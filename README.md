## 🧠 What is this?

Ever tried selling your car and heard:

> “Bhai last price bolo…”

Yeah… not anymore.

**Car-Value-AI** uses Machine Learning to predict the **fair market value** of your car in seconds.
No drama. No guesswork. Just data.



## ⚙️ Features

🔥 Instant price prediction
🚘 Supports multiple car brands
📊 Data-driven ML model
🎨 Clean dashboard UI
⚡ Fast & lightweight
